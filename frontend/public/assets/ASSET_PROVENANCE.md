- `global/millennia-hero-v1.png`: original concept art generated with OpenAI built-in ImageGen; not a historical source image.
- `global/chapter-atlas-v1.png`: original visual index generated with OpenAI built-in ImageGen; not an artifact, person, or site photograph.
- `han/han-caravan-v1.png`: original Han chapter atmosphere art generated with OpenAI built-in ImageGen; schematic and not a reconstruction of an exact historical itinerary.
- `han/han-route-scene-v2.png`: original photorealistic Han gameplay environment generated with OpenAI built-in ImageGen; an historically suggestive Hexi-corridor landscape, not a historical photograph, exact site reconstruction, or precise route record.
- `qing/qing-migration-v1.png`: original Qing chapter atmosphere art generated with OpenAI built-in ImageGen; the luminous path indicates narrative direction, not a precise migration route.
- `qing/qing-migration-photoreal-v2.png`: original photorealistic Qing gameplay environment generated with OpenAI built-in ImageGen; a historically suggestive late-18th-century steppe migration setting, not documentary photography, an exact Torghut route, or an identifiable historical moment.
- `contemporary/qiang-workshop-v1.png`: original contemporary workshop atmosphere art generated with OpenAI built-in ImageGen; anonymous hands and project-original study patterns, not documentary photography.
- `contemporary/qiang-workshop-photoreal-v2.png`: original photorealistic contemporary workshop setting generated with OpenAI built-in ImageGen; not documentary photography, not a record of a specific inheritor or workshop, and not evidence for any traditional motif.
- `contemporary/cocreation-sample-v1.png`: project-original AI-assisted demo result; must always be labelled `AI辅助文化创意作品 · 非传统羌绣原作` and `演示保障样例 · 非本次实时生成`.
